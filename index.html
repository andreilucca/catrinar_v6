Guiding question

That question sits underneath Mihai's work in classrooms, professional learning, movement, and Outdoor Joy.

Biography

Learning that is embodied, transferable, and deeply human.

Mihai Catrinar is an Education and Experiential Learning Leader whose practice connects curriculum, coaching, movement, and reflection. His work spans school leadership, physical and health education, concept-based learning, professional learning, and outdoor experiences that invite people to notice what they can carry back into everyday life.

His academic preparation includes an MA in Technology, Education and Learning from the University of Leeds, Leading Learning from Harvard Business School Online, and a BA in Physical Education. Across schools and workshops, he works to create experiences that are clear enough to transfer and alive enough to remember.
